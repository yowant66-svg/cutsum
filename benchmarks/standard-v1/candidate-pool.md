# Standard benchmark candidate pool

This is a maintainers' evidence-based selection record, not a popularity ranking. Selection
balances source stability, transcript or media availability, content structure, rights boundary,
and cross-item diversity. Rights notes describe the local test boundary and are not legal advice.
